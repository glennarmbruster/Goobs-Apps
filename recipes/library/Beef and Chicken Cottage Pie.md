# Beef + Chicken Breast Cottage Pie with Mushrooms

Serves 6-8. Filling can be fully made ahead (see Lunch Prep below) — dinner is just mash + assemble + bake.

## Ingredients

**Filling**
- 1 lb ground beef, 85/15 or 88/12
- 1 lb ground chicken breast
- 16 oz mushrooms, finely chopped
- 3-5 tablespoons jarred minced garlic
- 2 tablespoons granulated onion
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1 tablespoon olive oil (or reserved beef fat)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2-3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce (use 3 if skipping Better Than Bouillon)
- 1 tablespoon coconut aminos
- 1 3/4 cups low-sodium beef broth
- 3/4 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon Better Than Bouillon beef, if you have it
- 3 tablespoons flour (bumped up from 2 — see note)
- 1 tablespoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon Trader Joe's Umami seasoning
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar

**Mashed Potato Topping**
- 2 1/2 to 3 lb potatoes (golds, or half golds/half russets)
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 1/2 cup skim milk, plus more as needed
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

**A note on the flour:** the original called for 2 tablespoons, but between 2 1/2 cups of broth and the moisture 16 oz of mushrooms and 2 lbs of meat will release, that's likely to run thin. 3 tablespoons gets you closer to a gravy that clings to the meat and potatoes. If it's still loose after simmering, whisk 1 teaspoon cornstarch into 1 tablespoon cold water and stir it in for the last few minutes.

---

## Lunch Prep (Make-Ahead) — do this at lunch, dinner just needs mash + assembly

1. **Finely chop 16 oz mushrooms.** Fridge in a covered container.
2. **Peel and cube 2 1/2 to 3 lb potatoes.** Submerge in cold water in a covered container in the fridge so they don't brown.
3. **Shred 1/2 cup cheddar** if it's not pre-shredded.
4. **Build the full filling, start to finish:**
   - Melt 3 tablespoons butter and 1 tablespoon olive oil (or reserved beef fat) in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
   - Add the chopped mushrooms and cook until they release their liquid and it evaporates, 6-8 minutes.
   - Add 1 lb ground beef and 1 lb ground chicken breast. Break up and brown, 6-8 minutes.
   - Stir in 3-5 tablespoons jarred minced garlic, 2 tablespoons granulated onion, and 2 tablespoons tomato paste. Cook 1-2 minutes until the paste darkens slightly.
   - Sprinkle in 3 tablespoons flour and stir to coat everything evenly. Cook 1 minute to take the raw flour taste off.
   - Add 1 3/4 cups beef broth, 3/4 cup chicken broth, 2-3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce, 1 tablespoon coconut aminos, and 1 tablespoon Better Than Bouillon beef (if using).
   - Stir in 1 tablespoon dried thyme, 1 teaspoon TJ's Umami seasoning, 2 teaspoons smoked paprika, and 1 teaspoon black pepper.
   - Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until thick enough to hold a trail when you drag a spoon through it — 12-15 minutes. Thin with a splash of broth or thicken with the cornstarch slurry as needed.
   - Off heat, stir in 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar. Taste and adjust salt if needed.
   - Cool uncovered for 20-30 minutes, then cover and refrigerate.

This is the single biggest time-saver — cottage pie filling holds and reheats beautifully, so nothing is lost by making it 6+ hours ahead.

---

## Dinner (Assembly)

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Boil the pre-cubed potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, 15-18 minutes.
3. Drain and mash with 4 tablespoons butter, 4 oz cream cheese, 1/2 cup skim milk, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper. Add more milk as needed for a smooth, spreadable (not stiff) mash. Stir in 1/2 cup shredded cheddar.
4. Reheat the filling on the stovetop or microwave until hot throughout, then spread it into a 9x13 baking dish (or two smaller dishes if freezing one for later).
5. Top with the mashed potatoes, spreading to the edges to seal in the filling. Rough up the surface with a fork for extra browning.
6. Bake 20-25 minutes, until the filling bubbles at the edges and the potato top is golden. For deeper browning, broil the last 2-3 minutes.
7. Rest 5-10 minutes before serving.

**Freezer note:** this filling freezes well on its own — worth doubling the filling batch and freezing half for a near-instant cottage pie later (just needs fresh mash).
