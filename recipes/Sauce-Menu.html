General Tso’s Garlic-Chile Sauce14 min12434

A dark, glossy Chinese-American takeout sauce balances toasted mild chile, fresh ginger and a small, sharp splash of rice vinegar.

Lower-sodium batch. Use 1 tsp coconut aminos in place of 5 tsp.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp avocado oil
  • 2 fresh garlic cloves Peel and finely grate unless the step specifies otherwise.
  • 1/2 tsp ground ancho chile Use pure mild ancho, without added salt.
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger juice Finely grate peeled ginger and squeeze out the juice.
  • 3/4 cup low-sodium chicken broth Use plain homemade or unsalted carton broth at about 35 mg sodium per cup, with household-approved ingredients.
  • 5 tsp coconut aminos1 tsp coconut aminos Use Coconut Secret Original at 90 mg sodium per teaspoon.
  • 2 tbsp dark brown sugar Lightly packed.
  • 4 tsp unseasoned rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp smooth no-salt-added tomato paste
  • 2 tsp cornstarch
  • 4 tsp cold sodium-free water

Directions

  1. Bloom the chile. Warm avocado oil in the HexClad over medium-low heat. Stir in fresh garlic cloves and ground ancho chile for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Build the glaze. Whisk in fresh ginger juice, low-sodium chicken broth, coconut aminos, dark brown sugar, unseasoned rice vinegar and smooth no-salt-added tomato paste. Simmer uncovered for 4 minutes until the sugar dissolves and the color deepens.
  3. Mix the slurry. Stir cornstarch into cold sodium-free water in a small cup until smooth.
  4. Thicken the sauce. Whisk the slurry into the gently simmering base. Cook for 60–90 seconds, stirring, until the sauce coats the back of a spoon.
  5. Check the yield. Measure the sauce in a heatproof cup. Aim for 1 cup. If short, loosen with a little hot sodium-free water. If over, return it to the pan and simmer briefly to the mark.
  6. Serve the sauce. Spoon the warm sauce over moist chicken thighs or steamed cauliflower. Pass extra ground chile at the table.

Make ahead. Make the sauce the day before and portion it before chilling. Refrigerate in shallow covered portions within 2 hours and use within 3 days. Freezer-Friendly Verdict: Yes.
