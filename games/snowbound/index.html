How to play

  • Launch & Glide: drag down on the mountain and release, or set power and tap Launch. Upgrade your sled after runs; Rocket level 3 installs dual thrusters.
  • Endless Ride: practice steering and flight. Impacts cost hearts; missing a bridge or landing ends the run.
  • Drag left or right to steer. The rolling terrain and ramps launch you automatically. Tap Flip while high in the air; finish the rotation before landing for a small momentum bonus. Boost fires the rocket; ramps launch you into the air.
  • Collect stars for a powerup: star trails earn upgrades; periodic special stars refill your rocket.
  • Finish each course in one run with its required sled upgrades. Training runs earn upgrade tokens; distances do not add together. Twenty route designs grow from 7,500 to 10,000 m and add slaloms, chicanes, bridges, gaps and snowplows. After route 20, levels keep going with new scenery, colors and safe obstacle variations. Difficulty and upgrade requirements stop increasing.
  • Keyboard: ← → or A/D to steer, Space to flip, B for boost. Pause is at the top.
