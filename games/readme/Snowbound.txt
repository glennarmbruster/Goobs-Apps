SNOWBOUND 1.1 ALPHA 1 — reference-video and graphics pass

Upload the ZIP contents at the Goobs Games root, preserving folders. Include courses.js. This package also updates Spring Run to 0.6.0; no other games change.

Reviewed the supplied Sled Surfers recordings for their rolling ice slopes, long glides, changing scenic districts, bright roadside landmarks and camera movement.

Gameplay: stronger rolling terrain, less ground friction, longer upgraded glides, smoother airborne steering, reduced impact loss and a small momentum reward for completed flips. Camera follows height and turns more smoothly from a slightly higher viewpoint. Visible runner trails, peripheral speed streaks and broader swept wings reinforce motion.

Scenery/graphics: scenic districts change along each run, with snow villages, blue forests, crystal gardens and harbor/castle areas. Larger layered trees have snow on their boughs; added igloos, snowmen and low snowdrifts. Chalets have visible windows/doors on both faces and snow-covered roofs. Ice and snow have generated surface textures, with changing ice bands and quieter cracks. The approved icon stays. No towering mountain walls or pull-back instruction.

Progression remains 20 named base routes followed by unlimited numbered tours with bounded variations. Upgrades cap at 12 in each of five categories. Each course still needs its required upgrades and completion in one run. Saved progress, upgrades and tokens remain compatible. Exit stays in the pause menu during play.

Verification: automated actual-physics steering/boost runs finished all 20 base courses and levels 21–23 with a fully upgraded sled. Progression, save migration, upgrade gating, orientation, pause, textured mesh UVs and runner-trail updates checked. Software-rendered scene previews reviewed. Browser/WebGL appearance, performance and on-device playability have not been verified in this environment; device playtesting is still needed.
