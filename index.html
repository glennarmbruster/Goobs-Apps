Goobs Apps

Pick an app. Each one installs separately to your home screen and works offline.

BillsWhat's due, mark it paid, it rolls to the next date
MoneyRetirement Monte Carlo planner and monthly budget
TripsPacking, itinerary, budget, ideas and weather
GroceryShared list for two phones, grouped by aisle
To-doShared lists with due dates and notes
RecipesYour recipe collection with scaling, timers and cook mode

Bills and Money keep their data only on the device you enter it on; use their Backup screen to move it. Recipes keep your own additions on the device and read the shared collection from the site. Trips, Grocery and To-do work on their own too, and can be shared between two phones if you sign in.
